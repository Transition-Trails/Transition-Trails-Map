None of them is short of knowledge when they arrive, and none of them is placed because of what they learned. Every one is placed — or promoted, or trusted with a client — because of something they can show: a decision they defended, a system they inherited, a release they owned, a campaign they built. The pathway is not a curriculum with a certificate at the end. It is a machine for producing evidence.
