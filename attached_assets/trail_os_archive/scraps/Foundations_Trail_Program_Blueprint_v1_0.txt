1. Program Overview
1.1 What Is the Foundations Trail?
The Foundations Trail (FT) is a 12-week, self-guided program designed for learners who are working toward the Salesforce Administrator certification and want to build real project experience at the same time. It is the second step on the Transition Trails learning path — after the Explorer's Trail and before the Guided Trail.

The Foundations Trail does not just prepare you for the exam. It builds the portfolio that proves you can do the work. By the end of 12 weeks, you will have passed — or be ready to pass — the Salesforce Admin exam, and you will have a complete, published Salesforce build that employers can review.

This is not a study guide. It is a practicum with exam prep embedded inside it.

The Foundations Trail Difference: Build While You Study
Trailhead prepares you for the exam — but it doesn’t give you a project to show.
Bootcamps build things — but they often skip the exam or charge $5,000+ to do both.
The Foundations Trail does both at once, at a fraction of the cost.
Every Sprint builds a new layer of the HCH capstone AND maps directly to the Admin exam domains. By the time you sit the exam, you’ve already built the thing they’re testing you on.

1.4 The Penny AI Assistant
Penny is your on-demand AI learning partner throughout the Foundations Trail. She is available 24/7 inside the Academy platform and is specifically tuned to support the dual goal of this program: pass the exam AND build something real.

In the Foundations Trail, Penny shifts between two modes depending on what you need:

Exam Mode: Concept explanation, scenario quiz, mock exam questions, and 'explain it back to me' prompts that mirror the way the Admin exam actually tests knowledge.
Build Mode: Build reviewer that connects your HCH configuration work to the exam domain it belongs to, decision log partner that pushes back on weak reasoning, and stakeholder language coach that helps you explain technical decisions in plain English.

What Penny Is Not
Penny does not take the exam for you or walk you through build tasks step by step.
Penny does not replace your coach. She prepares you for more productive coaching conversations.
Penny is not a study guide that summarizes what to memorize. She is a thinking partner that helps you understand why — which is what the Admin exam actually tests.

3. The HCH Capstone — Foundations Trail Edition

Your capstone project is the Horizon Community Hub (HCH) — a realistic nonprofit that serves vulnerable communities in the Dallas-Fort Worth area. HCH provides utility assistance, housing stabilization support, and veteran services to families in crisis.

In the Foundations Trail, you are building HCH's Salesforce environment from scratch. You start with an empty Developer Edition org. 8. Salesforce Admin Exam Strategy
8.1 The Foundations Trail Approach to Exam Prep
Most exam prep programs treat the Admin exam as a memorization challenge. Transition Trails treats it as a comprehension challenge — because that is how the exam is actually designed. The Salesforce Admin exam is scenario-based. It does not ask you to define what a Profile is. It asks you to decide which Profile configuration solves a specific business problem.

The Foundations Trail prepares you for scenario-based thinking by building scenarios — real ones, in a real org, with real stakeholder context. By the time you sit the exam, you will have configured every major domain at least once inside HCH.

8.2 Penny Mock Exam Protocol
Penny runs structured mock exam sessions throughout the Foundations Trail. The protocol escalates by Sprint:

Sprint 1 (Weeks 1–4): 5-question mini-quiz after each build task, focused on the domain covered that week.
Sprint 2 (Weeks 5–8): 10-question domain quizzes after each build. Penny begins asking cross-domain scenario questions.
Sprint 3 (Weeks 9–12): Full 40-question mock exam in Week 11. Review all missed questions with Penny before sitting the real exam.

The goal is not to simulate the exam interface. It is to build the scenario-reasoning muscle that the exam rewards. Every Penny session in this program is designed to make the exam feel like a familiar problem format — not a surprise.

9.2 Grant & Funding Positioning
The Foundations Trail is the pipeline and preparation tier for Transition Trails’ workforce development grant strategy. Its grant narrative is built on two angles:

Exam outcome: % of graduates passing the Salesforce Admin exam within 90 days of program completion.
Pipeline metric: % of graduates proceeding into the Guided Trail (the direct employment outcome tier) within one cycle.

Employment outcomes for grant purposes are claimed at Guided Trail exit, not Foundations Trail exit. The Foundations Trail is framed to funders as the preparation investment that makes the Guided Trail outcome possible.

Scholarship pricing ($497) is set at a level that qualifies for WIOA Individual Training Accounts and community foundation scholarship pools. Full program cost ($997) is set to be grant-fundable at 1–2 scholarship seats per funder cycle.

A Final Note on Why the Foundations Trail Exists
The Salesforce Admin certification has one of the highest ROIs of any technology credential you can earn.
And yet, thousands of people who want it never get there — because they study in isolation, fail the exam once, and give up.
The Foundations Trail exists to close that gap.
Not by making the exam easier. By making sure that by the time you sit it, you’ve already built everything it tests.
You built a real nonprofit Salesforce org. You documented 12 configuration decisions. You explained your work to a stakeholder and a coach.
The exam is not the hardest thing you’ll do in the next 12 weeks. It’s the paperwork at the end.
