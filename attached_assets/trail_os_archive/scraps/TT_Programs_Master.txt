Our Programs at a Glance
Transition Trails runs two parallel program tracks: a learner pathway for individuals building or advancing Salesforce careers, and a client program for nonprofit organizations that need technology support. These programs are designed to feed each other — learners gain real-world experience by doing client work, and nonprofit clients benefit from coached, supervised support at a fraction of consulting rates.
This document is the single source of truth for all five programs as we move into launch. Use it to align on who we serve, what we offer, what it costs, and how the programs connect.
The Learner Pathway
Learners move through the pathway based on where they are in their Salesforce journey:
Explorer's Trail (ET) — Ongoing subscription for anyone exploring or practicing Salesforce. No prerequisites. The entry point and evergreen layer.
Foundations Trail (FT) — 12-week self-guided program for learners working toward the Salesforce Administrator certification. Build real projects while you study.
Guided Trail (GT) — 12-week cohort program for certified admins who aren't yet hired. Turn your cert into proof of readiness through real client-style work.
Trail of Mastery (ToM) — Advanced cohort for employed admins who want to move into consulting-level work. Real nonprofit client projects, coached delivery.
The Client Program
Digital Compass (DC) serves nonprofit organizations directly — not individual learners. Nonprofit clients become members, get structured Salesforce and technology support, and their projects are completed by coached GT and ToM learners. This creates a virtuous cycle: learners get real-world experience, nonprofits get high-quality support.
The Shared Capstone: Horizon Community Hub (HCH)
FT and GT learners build and administer a shared fictional nonprofit called Horizon Community Hub (HCH) — a Dallas-Fort Worth organization providing utility assistance, housing stabilization, and veteran services. This shared environment creates continuity: FT learners build the org from scratch; GT learners inherit it and work as ongoing admins under real stakeholder pressure from a character named Elena (Program Director).
Penny AI
Penny is Transition Trails' on-demand AI learning partner, embedded across all programs. Penny's role shifts by program: self-selection guide in ET, dual exam/build coach in FT, employment and career translator in GT, and guidance support in DC. All learners have access to Penny 24/7 inside the Academy platform.

How to Use This Table
When someone reaches out, ask two questions: (1) Do they have a Salesforce certification? (2) Are they currently employed in a Salesforce role? Those two answers determine the right program. If they represent a nonprofit organization looking for technology help, that's Digital Compass — a completely separate track.
Decision Flow
No cert, exploring → Explorer's Trail
Working toward cert → Foundations Trail
Has cert, not yet hired → Guided Trail
Employed admin, wants to advance → Trail of Mastery
Nonprofit org needs tech support → Digital Compass

ET — Explorer's Trail
&quot;You don't need permission to start. You just need a trail.&quot;

What It Is
The Explorer's Trail is a subscription-based, self-guided learning membership — the entry point and evergreen learning layer of the Transition Trails ecosystem. It is designed for anyone who wants structured, affordable, community-supported access to Salesforce curriculum without the commitment of a cohort-based program. There is no fixed end date and no prerequisites.
Who It Serves
The Undecided: Exploring whether Salesforce is the right career path. Needs low-stakes exposure to real concepts without a multi-week commitment.
The Accidental Admin: Already using Salesforce at work, never formally trained. Needs structured curriculum that fills the gaps their job created.
The Career Pivoter: Ready to change careers and building skills on their own timeline. Needs an affordable learning habit that compounds over time.
Program Structure
Monthly Trail Missions are the core learning unit. Each month, new missions are released across four skill levels — Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, and Expert — so learners always have something at the right level. Missions are self-contained and work with documentation, Penny, and the community. No walkthrough videos: learners figure it out.
Monthly rhythm: complete a Trail Mission (or more), work through daily/weekly Challenges, engage with Penny, participate in the Slack community, and optionally attend live webinars.
Membership Tiers & Pricing
Monthly: $47/mo — full access, cancel anytime
Annual: $397/yr (~$33/mo) — saves $167 vs. monthly, plus archive access and advanced Penny features
Annual + Coaching: $597/yr — adds two scheduled private 1:1 coaching sessions
GT Graduate: Annual-level access, earned upon completing Guided Trail (no charge)
GT + ToM Graduate: Lifetime Annual-level access, earned upon completing both programs (no charge)
Key Features
Monthly Trail Missions across 4 skill levels (new set released 1st of each month)
Monthly Capstone Project
Daily, Weekly & Monthly Challenges
Penny AI — concept help, self-selection guidance, LinkedIn review (all tiers); advanced Career Builder at Annual

Async 1:1 Coaching — submit a question, coach responds within 48 business hours
Live and recorded webinars (2-4/month)
Archive access and cumulative HCH-style build (Annual tiers only)
50% off Digital Compass / Nonprofit Consulting Program (Annual tiers)
Discounts on GT, FT, workshops, and bootcamps
Success Targets

Monthly Renewal Rate: 60%+ after first billing cycle

FT — Foundations Trail
&quot;The certification is the door. The Foundations Trail is how you prove you can walk through it.&quot;

What It Is
The Foundations Trail is a 12-week, self-guided program for learners working toward the Salesforce Administrator certification. It is a practicum with exam prep embedded inside it — learners build a real Salesforce environment (Horizon Community Hub) from scratch while studying for the exam. By the time they sit the exam, they've already built everything it tests.
Who It Serves
The Career Pivoter: Making a deliberate career change into Salesforce. Brings motivation and transferable business skills. Needs structured program that builds exam readiness AND portfolio proof simultaneously.
The Accidental Admin: Already using Salesforce at work but never formally trained. Wants the credential to match their reality. Needs to formalize what they know and fill the gaps.

Not ready for FT: learners without the Platform Foundations certification (start with ET Beginner/Intermediate), learners who have already passed the Admin exam (go to GT), or anyone looking for a passive study experience — FT requires weekly builds.
Prerequisites
Salesforce Platform Foundations Certification — or currently enrolled and preparing for it
Commitment to weekly builds (this is a practicum, not a study course)
Program Structure
Three Sprints, each four weeks long. Each sprint produces a concrete deliverable.
Sprint 1 (Weeks 1–4): The Foundation — Data Model & Management, Security & Access, Platform Basics. Output: Complete HCH data model, role hierarchy, profiles, permission sets, OWD settings.
Sprint 2 (Weeks 5–8): The Engine — Automation, Service Cloud, Data Management. Output: Email-to-Case, Web-to-Case, record-triggered Flows, assignment rules, data import/export, duplicate management.
Sprint 3 (Weeks 9–12): The Proof — Reports & Dashboards, Portfolio Completion, Exam Readiness. Output: Published portfolio, complete Admin Decision Log (12+ entries), exam scheduled or passed.
Admin Decision Log
Every learner documents a minimum of 12 configuration decisions throughout the program (FT-01 through FT-12). Each entry includes: the business context, options considered, option chosen, and business justification. At least 2 entries must document mistakes or unexpected outcomes. All entries should be written in language a non-technical stakeholder could understand.
Coaching
2 private coaching sessions (45 minutes each)
Session 1 (Week 4): Build check-in, career clarity, Penny usage review
Session 2 (Week 10): Exam readiness check, portfolio review, next steps
Weekly Office Hours: 45-60 min live, optional, recorded
Penny AI — Dual Mode
Exam Mode: concept explanation, scenario quizzes, mock exam questions (escalating: mini-quizzes Sprint 1, domain quizzes Sprint 2, full 40-question mock in Week 11)
Build Mode: build reviewer, decision log partner, stakeholder language coach
Pricing
Standard: $997 one-time payment
Payment Plan: 3x $367/month ($1,101 total)
Scholarship: $497 — application required; qualifies for WIOA Individual Training Accounts and community foundation pools
Completion & Outcomes
Completion is defined by deliverables, not attendance. Learners must complete all 12 weekly build tasks, publish a complete portfolio, complete the Admin Decision Log (12+ entries), attend both coaching sessions, schedule or pass the exam, and publish a final Loom walkthrough.
Completion Badge: verifiable, LinkedIn-displayable — employers can contact TT to verify
Admin Exam Pass Rate target: 75%+ within 90 days of completion
Portfolio Completion Rate target: 80%+ by Week 12
GT Pipeline Rate target: 40%+ enrolling in Guided Trail within one program cycle
Learner NPS target: 50+

GT — Guided Trail
&quot;You earned the certification. Now earn the job.&quot;

What It Is
The Guided Trail is a 12-week, cohort-based practicum for learners who have passed the Salesforce Administrator certification but have not yet been hired into their first Salesforce admin role. The gap isn't knowledge — it's proof. Employers don't hire certifications; they hire people who can demonstrate judgment under real conditions. The Guided Trail builds that proof.
Who It Serves
The Career Starter: New to the workforce, ready to begin a digital career. Brings coachability and curiosity. Needs hands-on project experience and confidence in their own judgment.
The Career Shifter: Transitioning from operations, support, or management in another industry. Brings transferable business skills. Needs a bridge to translate past experience into Salesforce interview language.
Prerequisites
Must hold the Salesforce Administrator Certification
Must not yet be employed as a Salesforce Admin
Must commit to 12-week cohort experience with live weekly participation (recordings are backup, not replacement)
Program Structure
Three Sprints, each four weeks long. Cohort size: 8–12 learners (minimum 6 to run, maximum 12). One coach per cohort.
Sprint 1 (Weeks 1–4): The Identity Phase — Org Audit, Data Architecture, Security Review, Stakeholder Alignment. Learner inherits the HCH org and works as its ongoing administrator.
Sprint 2 (Weeks 5–8): The Efficiency Engine — Automated Intake, Flow Automation, Case Management, Service Cloud. Output: Email-to-Case, Web-to-Case, Flows, Assignment Rules, full case management workflow.
Sprint 3 (Weeks 9–12): The Accountability Phase — SLA Management, Reporting & Dashboards, Proactive Escalation, Org Handover Documentation. Culminates in Capstone Demo Day.
What Makes GT Different: Problem-First Learning
Learners receive a Stakeholder Brief from Elena (fictional Program Director) describing a business problem. They must design the solution before building it. There are no walkthrough videos. Campfire Sessions require learners to present their build and explain their decisions to peers and the coach. This is deliberate: employers want admins who can think, not just follow instructions.
Admin Decision Log
Minimum 12 documented decisions (GT-01 through GT-12). At least 3 entries must document mistakes and what the learner would do differently. At least 2 entries must explicitly reference stakeholder preferences or constraints. All entries in language Elena could read and understand.
Coaching
3 private coaching sessions (45 minutes each)
Session 1 (Week 4): How you are presenting your work — employer simulation with one artifact
Session 2 (Week 8): Portfolio mid-point review, interview readiness, resume and LinkedIn audit
Session 3 (Week 11): Final career clarity, employer simulation mock interview
Weekly Stand-Ups: 20-minute group check-in each week
Weekly Campfire Sessions: 60-minute live group discussion with coach
Capstone Demo Day (Week 12)
Each learner presents their HCH build in an 8-minute presentation followed by a 4-minute Q&A. Guests from the Salesforce professional community are invited. This is the culminating public demonstration of readiness.
Pricing
Standard: $1,497 one-time payment
Payment Plan: 3x $549/month
Scholarship: $747 — application required
At $1,497 vs. a $73K average entry-level Salesforce Admin salary, payback is under one week of the learner's first paycheck.
Completion & Outcomes
Completion is defined by deliverables, not attendance. Required: all 12 weekly build tasks, published portfolio with all required artifacts, Admin Decision Log (12+ GT entries), all 3 coaching sessions, Demo Day presentation, and Career Story section (3-5 curated highlights with problem/solution/business outcome language).
Post-completion: TT provides a portfolio review and job search guidance session for all GT graduates
Employment Conversion Rate target: % hired as Salesforce Admins within 6 months of completion
Cohort Retention Rate target: 85%+
Learner NPS target: 50+

ToM — Trail of Mastery
&quot;From credentialed to capable. From capable to indispensable.&quot;

What It Is
The Trail of Mastery is the advanced cohort for employed Salesforce Admins who want to move into consulting-level, practitioner work. Instead of a fictional capstone environment, ToM participants work on real nonprofit client projects sourced through the Digital Compass program. Projects are delivered under direct expert coach guidance.
This is the program that closes the gap between holding a certification and being trusted with real, complex, client-facing work — and it creates a direct pipeline between learners and Digital Compass nonprofit clients.
Who It Serves
Certified Admins needing client experience: Have the credential, want to prove they can deliver at a higher level in real-world conditions.
Certified Business Analysts: Need real-world client-facing experience in that role.
Technical professionals seeking advancement: Aiming for Admin → Product Owner or Advanced Administrator transitions.
Prerequisites
Currently employed as a Salesforce Admin
Skills assessment may be required to confirm readiness for program rigor
Natural pathway from Guided Trail, though not a hard requirement
Program Structure
Cohort-based, ongoing. Participants work in teams on real nonprofit client projects submitted through the Digital Compass portal. Weekly Campfire Sessions plus weekly client sessions. Expert coach guidance included throughout.
Real client projects: sourced from DC nonprofit member organizations
Weekly Campfire Sessions + weekly client sessions
Expert coaching included
Participants earn a completed project portfolio and documented case studies
ToM participants take on DC client projects with greater complexity and autonomy than GT learners
Pricing

Employer-Sponsored Groups (3+ seats): $1,597/seat — opens B2B channel from L&D budgets
Payment Plan: 4x $549/month
Scholarship: Availability TBD — funded via grant revenue, not program margin

DC — Digital Compass
&quot;Real Help for the Person Who Didn't Ask for This Job&quot;

What It Is
Digital Compass is Transition Trails' nonprofit client technology program. It serves nonprofit organizations directly — not individual learners. It provides structured Salesforce guidance, technology training, and consulting project support for nonprofit professionals who need to use their tools more effectively but don't have dedicated technology staff.
Digital Compass is a membership — a recurring relationship between Transition Trails and the nonprofit organizations they serve. Projects submitted through DC are completed by coached GT and ToM learners, creating a direct connection between the learner pathway and the client program.
Who It Serves
The Accidental Admin: Staff member who inherited Salesforce without formal training — program manager, development officer, ED assistant — now also tech lead. Deep knowledge of mission and data, just not the platform it lives in.
The Nonprofit Leader: Executive Director or COO who knows their org needs to use technology better but doesn't know where to start or who to trust. Has strategic vision and organizational authority.
The Emerging Organization: Early-stage nonprofit building their Salesforce instance from the ground up with limited resources and no tech background.
Prerequisites
None — open to any nonprofit organization with a Salesforce org or technology need. Nonprofit registration is required before project submission (verifies nonprofit status and establishes the technical Trail OS relationship).
Membership Tiers
Entry Tier: $149/mo or $1,490/yr — Weekly Office Hours, TrailKit library, Trail OS installation, Penny AI, Slack community
Mid Tier: $349/mo or $3,490/yr — All Entry features plus Annual Strategy Review (Angela), Annual Architecture Review (Hugh & Kim), personalized technology roadmap, project submission access
Full Tier: $749/mo or $7,490/yr — All Mid features plus recurring guided implementation and ongoing support, priority project queue placement, dedicated TT point of contact
Annual pricing = 10x monthly (2 months free). All tiers available on annual invoicing — recommended for grant-funded purchases.
Core Services
Weekly Office Hours: live, open-door sessions for real Salesforce and tech questions
TrailKits: self-serve digital resource packages (guides, templates, AI prompt packs for Claude/Gemini/GPT, curated nonprofit discounts). Available: Nonprofit Salesforce Starter Kit, AI Central Command for Nonprofits, Enabling AI in Salesforce, Data Hygiene Playbook, DKIM Email Deliverability Guide
Fix It With Me Videos: screen-recorded walkthroughs of common Salesforce problems
Build With Me Videos: step-by-step configuration walkthroughs

Project Support (Mid/Full): projects submitted through portal, completed by coached GT/ToM learners

The goal is for members to report improved confidence in at least 3 pillars within 12 months.
