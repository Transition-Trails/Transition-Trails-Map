SECTION 1 — WHY TRAIL OS IS THE MEASUREMENT INFRASTRUCTURE
## The Problem Trail OS Solves
Most workforce development programs cannot answer the question funders actually ask: 'How do you know it worked?' They have completion rates. They have satisfaction scores. They occasionally have employment rates — if they tracked them manually.
What they cannot do is connect an employment outcome back to a specific program element and say: participants who completed this capstone build at this quality level in this track were 40% more likely to be employed within 90 days than participants who didn't. That level of specificity requires a data model that was designed for measurement from the start — not retrofitted onto a spreadsheet after the fact.
Trail OS was designed for measurement from the start. Every object, every relationship, every field is intentional. The Demand Management pipeline creates an audit trail for every build. The Skills Assessment Framework creates a competency record for every learner. The Survey Model creates an outcomes record at four points in every participant's journey. Combined, these three data sources answer every question a funder, employer, or program designer needs to ask.

Why Program Version Tagging Is Non-Negotiable
When GT v2.0 produces better outcomes than GT v1.0, we need to know which specific changes drove the improvement. Was it the two-track model? The RESOLVE pipeline introduction? With version tagging, we can run a report and know within one cohort cycle.

## Navigator + DC Client Hybrid Survey
For a volunteer admin or nonprofit staff member who is both a DC membership participant and a Navigator in a TT program — the survey combines both instruments with shared questions consolidated. Penny identifies this dual status from the Trail OS enrollment record and triggers the hybrid instrument automatically. No duplicate surveys.
How Penny Handles the Hybrid
When a JotForm survey trigger fires, Penny checks the participant's Trail OS record for both TT_Enrollment__c (Navigator status) and TT_Client_Project__c (DC Client status). If both exist, the hybrid survey link is sent instead of the standard Navigator survey. The hybrid survey captures both dimensions in a single 10–12 minute instrument. Responses are stored as two linked TT_Survey_Response__c records — one tagged Navigator, one tagged DC Client — so reporting works correctly for both lenses.
SECTION 4 — JOTFORM ARCHITECTURE & SALESFORCE INTEGRATION
## Why JotForm, Not Salesforce Survey
FOR: Hugh — licensing check required before build
Factor
The Reality
What TT has in Salesforce Survey
Enterprise Edition includes the basic Survey Response Pack: 1,000 responses, standard question types, simple branching. No merge fields. No record updates. No embedded surveys. Survey data sits in survey objects and does not automatically update any other record.
What unlocking it costs
Feedback Management Starter (next tier) is approximately $46,000/month. That tier adds merge fields, basic embedding, and sentiment analysis. Not a realistic option for TT's current stage.
The structural problem
Even at higher tiers, Salesforce Survey does not write responses back to custom objects like TT_Survey_Response__c without significant custom development. It was designed for satisfaction surveys, not program measurement systems.
What JotForm does instead
JotForm's native Salesforce integration maps form fields directly to any Salesforce object — including custom objects. A completed Navigator survey creates a TT_Survey_Response__c record automatically, linked to the correct Enrollment, tagged with the Survey Moment, with zero manual data entry.
The TT org assessment connection
We already use JotForm. The MCP connector is live in Claude. Hugh knows the integration. This is not a new vendor — it is extending an existing tool we already trust.
Hugh's action item
Check TT's current JotForm plan for: response limits, Salesforce integration tier availability, and field mapping capabilities for custom objects. If the current plan doesn't support Salesforce custom object mapping, upgrade before the survey build begins.
## How JotForm Connects to Trail OS
The integration flow for every survey response:
Step 1 — TRIGGER: Trail OS fires a survey trigger at the correct moment (Pre-Program = Week 1 enrollment, Mid = Week 4, Completion = Week 12, 90-Day = 90 days after TT_Enrollment__c.Status = Completed). Trigger can be a Flow on TT_Enrollment__c or a Penny proactive Slack message.
Step 2 — PERSONALIZATION: The survey link includes a hidden prefill parameter containing the participant's Enrollment ID (TT_Enrollment__c.Id). JotForm receives this parameter and stores it as a hidden field in the response.
Step 3 — RESPONSE: Participant completes the JotForm survey. On submission, JotForm's Salesforce integration fires.
Step 4 — MAPPING: JotForm creates a new TT_Survey_Response__c record in Salesforce. The Enrollment ID from the hidden field populates the TT_Enrollment__c lookup. The Survey Instrument and Survey Moment fields are populated from the form's configuration. Every survey question maps to a specific field on TT_Survey_Response__c.
Step 5 — ROLLUP: Formula and roll-up fields on TT_Enrollment__c and TT_Cohort__c aggregate the response data for reporting. Angela's dashboard updates automatically.

SECTION 5 — THE METRICS DASHBOARD
## What Gets Measured and Who Sees It
Trail OS produces four dashboard views. Each is designed for a specific audience and a specific decision. None requires manual data assembly.
FOR: Angela — Program Performance + Grant Metrics
Metric
Definition & Source
Employment Rate (90-Day)
% of Navigator completions who report employment in a Salesforce role within 90 days. Source: TT_Survey_Response__c (90-Day Moment). Filterable by program version, track, cohort, capstone version.
Wage Gain (Average)
Average salary increase from Pre-Program baseline to 90-Day follow-up. Source: TT_Survey_Response__c (Pre vs. 90-Day). Reportable by program version and cohort.
Domain Confidence Gain
Average improvement in skill confidence rating (1–5 scale) across 9 Admin exam domains. Source: TT_Survey_Response__c (Pre vs. Completion). The before/after story for grant applications.
RESOLVE Pipeline Completion Rate
% of enrollments where at least one full RESOLVE cycle (Case through Production deployment) was completed. Source: Trail OS Case + Work Item + TT_Enrollment__c link.
Portfolio Completion Rate
% of enrollments where portfolio was published by Week 12. Source: TT_Enrollment__c (portfolio_published__c flag set by coach).
Program Version Comparison
Side-by-side metric comparison between GT v1.0 and GT v2.0 (or any two versions). Answers: did the program changes improve outcomes? Requires version tagging on all records.
FOR: Kim — Program Operations + Client Health
Metric
Definition & Source
Cohort Health (Weekly)
Friday Gemini synthesis from Penny: momentum flags, clarity issues, confidence gaps by learner. Source: Penny interaction logs + TT_Assessment_Result__c. Kim sees the cohort-level pattern, not individual PII.
Coach Gate Status
How many learners are currently in advisory, blocking, or critical gate status across all active cohorts. Source: TT_Assessment_Result__c domain scores. Kim escalates to coaches.
DC Client NPS
Net Promoter Score across all active and completed DC client relationships. Source: TT_Survey_Response__c (Project Close Moment). Filterable by program version and client type.
Client Project Delivery Rate
% of DC client projects that delivered all scoped Work Items to production. Source: Trail OS Work Items with Date_Pushed_to_Prod__c set vs. total scoped.
Mid-Program Risk Flags
Learners who have not responded to the Mid-Program survey within 7 days of trigger. Source: TT_Survey_Response__c absence check. Kim follows up personally or via Penny.
Track Assignment Accuracy
At 90-Day follow-up, did participants report that their Solo Admin or Team track assignment felt right for their situation? Source: TT_Survey_Response__c (Completion Moment, track fit question).
FOR: Kel — Finance, Compliance & Grant Reporting
Metric
Definition & Source
Cost Per Employed Graduate
Total program cost ÷ number of graduates employed within 90 days. Requires: TT_Enrollment__c (cost field) + TT_Survey_Response__c (90-Day employment status). Reportable by program version.
ETPL Outcome Report
% employed + average starting salary + average wage gain, filterable by program and cohort. Formatted for Texas Workforce Commission ETPL reporting requirements.
TWC SDF Documentation
Wage increase documentation: Pre-Program salary vs. post-program salary, linked to specific participant records. Exportable as a compliance report.
Scholarship Utilization
# and % of scholarship seats used per cohort, by scholarship type. Source: TT_Enrollment__c (scholarship_type__c field).
Grant Metric Export
One-click export of all grant-required metrics for a specified cohort range and program version. Formatted for common funder report templates. Source: aggregated Trail OS + Survey data.
90-Day Response Rate
% of Navigators who complete the 90-Day follow-up survey. Low response rate = unreliable outcome data = weaker grant narrative. Kel monitors this and escalates to Kim if rate drops below 70%.
FOR: Hugh — System Health & Build Priorities
Metric
Definition & Source
JotForm Integration Health
# of survey submissions in last 7 days vs. expected based on active enrollments. Any gap = integration issue. Source: TT_Survey_Response__c CreatedDate vs. expected trigger dates.
Trail OS Pipeline Completeness
% of Work Items with all required fields populated: Case link, Sprint link, Date_Pushed_to_Prod__c, Acceptance Criteria Pass/Fail. Incomplete records = reporting gaps.
