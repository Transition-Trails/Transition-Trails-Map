## 1. Vision Statement
Trail OS is Transition Trails' proprietary operating platform — built natively on Salesforce and powered by Google Gemini — that enables a small founding team to deliver world-class workforce development programs, serve nonprofit clients at scale, and develop the next generation of Salesforce practitioners across six role tracks. It is simultaneously an internal operating system, a teaching laboratory, a client services platform, and an AI-powered coaching infrastructure. TT doesn't just build Trail OS for others. TT runs on Trail OS. The founders are learners too.
## The Problem Trail OS Solves
Transition Trails cannot scale through headcount. There are not enough certified Salesforce experts who can dedicate time to coaching, client services, and program delivery at the volume TT needs to serve its mission. The only path to scale is AI-augmented infrastructure that multiplies the impact of every human on the team — coaches, Trail of Mastery students, founders, and clients alike.
Trail OS is that infrastructure. Penny — TT's AI companion powered by Google Gemini and Salesforce Agentforce — serves every person in TT's ecosystem: learners, coaches, clients, job seekers, accidental admins, donors, and the founders themselves. The platform and the program are inseparable. Every tool TT uses is a tool TT teaches.
## The Long-Term Vision
Trail OS is designed to be offered beyond Transition Trails — to other nonprofits that use Salesforce or want to start. Organizations that are under-resourced, under-supported, and operating on technology that isn't serving their mission. TT is the first client. It will not be the last.

## Fixed Decisions — Not Up for Debate
These architectural and philosophical decisions are made. Features, sprints, and Program Increments must respect them.
Decision
What It Means
Salesforce-Native Platform
Trail OS is built natively on Salesforce. No exceptions. Every feature is a Salesforce object, Flow, or Agentforce agent — not a third-party integration that creates data silos.
Google Gemini as AI Engine
Penny's intelligence runs on Google Gemini (currently 2.5 Flash). The knowledge base is Google NotebookLM + Google Drive. Google Classroom is the LMS integration.
Human in the Loop — Always
No deployment, no client-facing message, no change request advances without a human review. This is a trust architecture, not a limitation.
RESOLVE as the Methodology
Every client engagement, every learner build, every change request runs through the RESOLVE framework. It is non-negotiable and proprietary.
Admin Cert as Baseline
Every Trail of Mastery participant holds the Salesforce Certified Platform Administrator credential. This is the entry requirement, not a preference.
Google Workspace Foundation
Every client engagement starts with Google Workspace for Nonprofits as the foundation layer — before Salesforce, before automation, before Trail OS capabilities.

## 9. Non-Negotiables
These principles are architectural and philosophical guardrails. They are not features to be prioritized — they are constraints that every feature must respect. When a product decision conflicts with a non-negotiable, the non-negotiable wins.
Principle
What It Means for Product Decisions
Human in the Loop
No deployment, no client-facing Penny message, no change request, and no major decision advances without a human review. This is the trust architecture. It is not negotiable and does not erode as the platform matures.
RESOLVE Methodology
Every client engagement and every learner build runs through RESOLVE. It is TT's proprietary framework. Trail OS features must support it, not replace it.
Salesforce-Native First
If a capability can be built natively in Salesforce, it is built natively. Third-party integrations are used only when no native option exists. This keeps the platform teachable.
Google Gemini as AI Engine
Penny's intelligence runs on Google Gemini. Architecture must support this — not default to generic Salesforce Einstein responses when Gemini is the designed engine.
Admin Cert Baseline
Trail of Mastery entry requires the Salesforce Certified Platform Administrator credential. No exceptions. The cert is the floor, not the ceiling.
Real Client Work Only
TT programs use real nonprofit clients, real data, and real stakeholders. Simulations, sandboxes, and case studies are not the product. If a feature supports simulation instead of real work, it is deprioritized.
Google Workspace First for Clients
Every nonprofit client engagement starts with Google Workspace foundation. Before Salesforce. Before automation. Before Trail OS. This is the technology onramp.
TT Is the First Client
