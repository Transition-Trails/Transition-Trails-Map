About This Guide
This guide documents the Knowledge capability within Trail OS — Transition Trails's Salesforce-based operating system. It covers the architecture, article types, data category structure, authoring and publishing workflow, tag taxonomy, object relationships, and governance standards.
Sections marked IN BUILD document the designed architecture for components not yet deployed. These sections are accurate as designed — they will not require rewriting when the component goes live, only a status update.
## 1. What Is the Knowledge Capability
The Knowledge capability is the structured content library within Trail OS. It is the single authoritative source for all operational procedures, program curriculum, Navigator resources, and published content at Transition Trails.
Knowledge is not a file storage system or a document library. It is a governed, searchable, role-aware content platform built on Salesforce Knowledge that connects directly to Penny (Agentforce), Experience Cloud, and the Demand Management pipeline.

## 1.2 What Knowledge Is Not
Not Google Drive. Active working documents, campaign files, Canva assets, and draft content stay in Google Drive. Knowledge is for finished, governed content only.
Not a file storage system. Videos, PDFs, and large files are hosted externally (Google Drive, Loom, YouTube). Knowledge holds the structured article that references and contextualizes those files.
Not a wiki. Every article goes through a governed authoring and publishing workflow. Nothing is ad hoc. Angela is the publisher for every article.
Not public by default. All articles are currently internal only. Visibility expands to Navigators, clients, and public audiences on a controlled schedule as the program matures.

## 1.4 How Knowledge Fits in Trail OS
Knowledge connects to four other Trail OS capabilities:
Demand Management
Every change request that results in a new or updated SOP closes with a link to the relevant Knowledge article. Bug Fix cases link to the article that documents the expected behavior. Training Issue cases route directly to Knowledge.
Agentforce & Penny
Penny uses the GetRelevantKnowledge action to surface articles to Navigators, coaches, and clients in real time. She routes by the Applies To field on SOPs and by Audience and Program Track tags on Curriculum Content.
Experience Cloud
Published articles are surfaced on the Navigator portal and (eventually) the public site. Visibility is controlled by Data Category settings per user profile.
Integrations
The Notion → Make → Salesforce nightly sync is the authoring pipeline. Notion is the draft environment. Make detects status changes and creates draft articles in Salesforce. Angela is the publish gate after sync.
## 2. Article Types
Trail OS uses two Knowledge record types. The record type determines the fields on the layout, the content structure, and how Penny routes the article. Choosing the wrong record type is the most common authoring error — it cannot be corrected without archiving and recreating the article.
The Decision Rule
If the article answers "how do I operate Trail OS or run this process," it is an SOP.
If the article answers "how do I learn this or do this as a Navigator or client," it is Curriculum Content.
When in doubt: who is the reader, and what are they trying to accomplish?
## 2.1 Article Type: SOP
SOP — Standard Operating Procedure   |   IN PRODUCTION
An SOP documents how to perform a specific operational task within Trail OS. It is written for someone who needs to complete a process — not someone who needs to learn a concept.
SOPs fall under all three Data Category groups, depending on their topic. They are authored in Notion, synced via Make, reviewed by Angela, and published to Salesforce. Every SOP has an owner department and a review frequency.

## 3. Data Categories
Data Categories are the classification and visibility engine for all Knowledge articles. They control who can see what, organize the library into a navigable hierarchy, and enable Penny to filter content by topic area. Salesforce limits active category groups to three. Trail OS uses all three.
Critical Rule
The Data Category on an article is the AREA classification. Do not create a separate Area picklist field on any article type — that is a duplicate. Data Category IS the area. Select it from the category tree when creating or editing any article.

## 4. Authoring & Publishing Workflow
Every Knowledge article at Transition Trails follows a governed workflow from first draft to live article. Angela is the human gate at the publish step — nothing goes live without her explicit action. This is enforced by the Salesforce workflow, not just policy.
## 4.1 Current Workflow — SOP Record Type
Current State — Manual Authoring Directly in Salesforce
While the Notion → Make → Salesforce sync is in build, SOPs are authored directly in Salesforce Knowledge. This is the current workflow for all four staff members.
Go to Service Console → Knowledge → New. Select SOP as the record type.
Set the Data Category. Select the correct category from the tree. This is the most important field — do not skip it or set it incorrectly. It cannot be changed after publication without admin access.
Set classification fields first. Owner Department, Applies To, Difficulty Level, Estimated Time, Review Frequency. Set these before writing content.
Write the content fields in order. Overview → Prerequisites → Procedure → Notes & Exceptions. Write Procedure as numbered steps. Embed screenshots inline using the rich text image button.
Add reference fields. Tango URL (if walkthrough exists), Related Articles with context notes, Last Tested Version.
Save as Draft. Status = Draft. Do not publish yet.
Notify Angela. Let Angela know the article is ready for review. She will edit if needed and publish.
Angela reviews and publishes. Angela opens the draft, reviews all fields, makes edits if needed, and clicks Publish. Status moves to Online. The article is live.
## 4.2 Future Workflow — Notion → Make → Salesforce Sync
IN BUILD — Nightly Notion → Make → Salesforce Sync
IN BUILD
The Notion → Make → Salesforce sync is designed and will replace the manual authoring workflow once deployed. The sync fields (Notion_Page_ID__c, Notion_Source_URL__c, Notion_Sync_Status__c) are already on the SOP layout in anticipation.
This section documents the designed workflow. It will be activated without requiring changes to the article layout or category structure.
Stage 1 — Draft in Notion
The author writes SOP in Notion using the standard template. Sets SF Data Category, SF Record Type = SOP, and Owner Department in Notion template fields.
Stage 2 — Ready for Review
Author sets Notion Status = Ready for Review. This is the human trigger that activates the nightly Make sync.
Stage 3 — Nightly Make Sync
Make automation runs nightly. Detects Status = Ready for Review. Creates a draft Knowledge article in Salesforce. Maps all content fields. Sets Notion Sync Status = Synced to SF - Pending Review.
Stage 4 — Angela Reviews
Angela opens the draft in Salesforce Knowledge. Reviews all four content fields. Checks Data Category, classification fields, Tango URL, and Estimated Time.
Stage 5 — Angela Edits (if needed)
Angela edits any field directly in Salesforce. Salesforce becomes the record of truth from this point onward. Notion holds the draft history only.
Stage 6 — Angela Publishes
Angela clicks Publish. Status → Online. Notion Sync Status → Published (Make updates automatically). This is the final human gate.
Stage 7 — Article Live
Article immediately visible to all internal Salesforce users. Penny can surface it. Experience Cloud visibility follows Data Category settings.
Stage 8 — Sync Failure Protocol
If Notion Sync Status = Sync Failed: check Make automation run history immediately. Log a Demand Management case with Change Type = Automation Change. Do not publish manually until the root cause is identified.
## 4.3 Review Cycle
Every published article has a review schedule defined by the Review Frequency field. Angela is responsible for the review cycle on all articles. The Next Review Date is calculated automatically from Last Reviewed Date + Review Frequency.
Monthly
Any SOP touching an active automation or integration. These break silently when platform updates occur.
Quarterly (Default)
All Salesforce configuration SOPs. Salesforce releases three times per year — config SOPs must be verified after each release.
Annually
Stable reference articles, governance documents, HR policies, and legal compliance documentation.
As Needed
Content library articles and Trail Kit resources that do not have a technical expiry. Review triggered by program changes, not calendar.

Every article must follow this naming convention. Consistent naming makes search results readable and is required for Penny to surface articles reliably.

## 5. Tag Taxonomy
IN BUILD — Tags configured after SOP custom fields are complete
Tags complement Data Categories. Categories answer where does this content live. Tags answer what else this content is about. A single article can carry tags from multiple dimensions, making it findable from multiple angles without duplicating it across categories.
Guided Tags — Controlled Vocabulary
Trail OS uses Guided Tags — the approved list is managed in Setup → Knowledge Settings → Article Tags. Only tags on the approved list can be applied to articles.
Do not create new tags without logging a Demand Management case. Tag sprawl degrades Penny's routing accuracy and makes the library harder to maintain.
Change Type for new tag requests: Process & Documentation → Process Change.

## 6. Object Relationships
Knowledge articles connect to other Trail OS objects. Understanding these connections is essential for maintaining data integrity, for closing Demand Management cases correctly, and for understanding how Penny finds and surfaces content.
## 6.1 Knowledge → Demand Management (Case)
The most important relationship in Trail OS. Every Demand Management case that results in new or changed content closes with a link to the relevant Knowledge article.
New SOP / Procedure case
When the SOP is published, link it in the Related Knowledge Article field on the case before closing. Status: Closed — Complete.
Process Change case
When the affected article is updated and republished, link it before closing. If multiple articles were updated, link the primary one and note the others in the Resolution Description.
Bug Report case
Two links: (1) the article documenting expected behavior that was violated, (2) any article updated as a result of the fix. Check Knowledge Update Needed before closing.
Training Issue case
Penny searches Knowledge and surfaces the top matching articles. The closing article is linked before the case closes as a Training Issue. If no article exists, a new SOP request is logged.
Client Request case
Client-specific articles can be linked to both the case and the client's Account record for future reference.
## 6.2 Knowledge → Account
Client-specific SOPs and Trail Kit resources can be linked to the relevant nonprofit Account record. This enables Penny to surface client-relevant content when working on a client Case and gives the account team a complete view of the documentation for each client.
When to Link an Article to an Account
When a SOP is written specifically for a client's Salesforce configuration, link it to their Account.
When a Trail Kit resource is customized for a specific client cohort, link it to the Account.
General best-practice guides and internal SOPs do not require Account links.
## 6.3 Knowledge → Opportunity
Best-practice guides and client-deliverable articles can be linked to open Opportunities during the pre-sales and discovery phases. This surfaces relevant content during client conversations and builds the engagement documentation package.
## 6.4 Knowledge → Penny (Agentforce)
IN BUILD — Penny GetRelevantKnowledge Action
IN BUILD
Penny's GetRelevantKnowledge action is designed and will be configured as part of the Agentforce & Penny capability build. The Knowledge architecture — article types, Applies To field, tag taxonomy — has been built to support Penny from Day 1.
This section documents the intended behavior so that staff understand how Knowledge articles should be structured to surface correctly in Penny.
Penny uses the GetRelevantKnowledge Agentforce action to find and surface articles in real time. She searches the title, summary, body, and tags. She filters by specific fields depending on the context:
For SOP articles
Penny filters by the Applies To field. A Navigator asking a question gets articles where Applies To includes Learner. A coach gets articles where "Applies To" includes "Coach". Internal staff get all articles.
For Curriculum Content
Penny filters by Audience tag and Program Track tag simultaneously. A Guided Trail Navigator gets content tagged as "Navigator + Guided-Trail". A Trail of Mastery student gets Trail-of-Mastery-Student content.
For Demand Management cases
Penny surfaces articles relevant to the Change Type. Training Issue cases get the top 3 matching SOPs. Bug Report cases receive the SOP that documents the expected behavior for the affected component.
For Client Request cases
Penny pulls the Account record first, surfaces prior cases for that client, then searches for articles tagged Client-Deliverable or Nonprofit-Client that match the request topic.
Tango URL surfacing
Penny can surface just the Tango_URL__c field without returning the full procedure text. Used when a Navigator needs the interactive walkthrough rather than the written steps.
What prevents Penny from finding an article
Notion Sync Status is not published. The Applies To field is empty on an SOP. No Audience tag on a Curriculum Content article. Naming convention not followed — title search returns poor matches.
## 6.5 Knowledge → Notion (Sync Pipeline)
IN BUILD — Notion Source Fields
The three Notion sync fields on the SOP article type create the connection between the Notion draft environment and Salesforce Knowledge as the published record of truth.
Notion_Page_ID__c
The dedup key. If this field is populated on an existing Salesforce article, the nightly Make sync updates the record instead of creating a duplicate. Set by Make at first sync. Never edit manually.
Notion_Source_URL__c
Links back to the original Notion draft. Allows Angela to click through to the draft history and authoring context. Populated automatically by Make.
Notion_Sync_Status__c
The state machine for the sync workflow. Values: Draft → Synced to SF - Pending Review → Published → Sync Failed. Managed by Make. Angela moves to Published by clicking Publish in Salesforce.

Trail OS Knowledge is only as useful as the discipline behind it. These are the standing rules that keep the library accurate, searchable, and trustworthy.
## 7.1 The Non-Negotiables
Angela is the only person who publishes Knowledge articles. No exceptions. Kim, Michele, and Hugh author and draft — Angela publishes. This is enforced by Salesforce workflow permissions.
No article is created without a Data Category assigned. A miscategorized article is worse than no article — it either surfaces to the wrong audience or disappears from search entirely.
No article is created without following the naming convention. Inconsistent naming breaks Penny's title search and makes the library un-navigable.
No new tags are created without a Demand Management request. Tag sprawl degrades Penny's routing accuracy. All new tags go through the approval process.
Notion sync fields are never edited manually. Notion_Page_ID__c, Notion_Source_URL__c, and Notion_Sync_Status__c are managed exclusively by Make automation. Manual edits break the dedup logic, creating duplicate articles.
Wrong article type = archive and recreate. You cannot change the record type on an existing Knowledge article. If the wrong type was used, archive the article and create a new one with the correct type. Do not attempt to work around this.

## 7.3 When Things Go Wrong
Sync Failed status appears
Check the Make automation run history immediately. Identify the error. Log a Demand Management case with Change Type = Automation Change. Do not publish the article manually until the root cause is resolved and the sync is confirmed to be working.
The article content is incorrect
Do not archive. Edit the article directly in Salesforce and republish. If the error was caused by a process failure, log the correction in Lessons Learned on the related DM case.
Wrong Data Category assigned
Correct it directly in the article. No DM case needed for metadata corrections. Notify Angela so she can republish.
Wrong article type used
Archive the incorrectly typed article. Create a new article with the correct record type. Re-enter all content. Link the new article to any related DM cases.
Duplicate article created
Keep the article with the lower Article Number (the one created first). Archive the duplicate. Update Notion_Page_ID__c on the surviving article if the sync created the duplicate.
Tag not in approved list
Use the closest matching approved tag. Log a Demand Management case to add the new tag if genuinely needed. Change Type: Process Change.
Angela is unavailable to publish
Articles stay in Draft until Angela is available. There is no backup publisher. Urgent operational needs are handled by direct Salesforce access — not by bypassing the publish gate.
