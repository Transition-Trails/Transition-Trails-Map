## SHEET: Penny Prompt Bank
Foundations Trail — Penny AI Prompt Bank (PB-F1) |  |  | 
These starter prompts are designed for Foundations Trail learners who don't yet know how to ask precise Salesforce questions. Instructions for learners: Copy the prompt, paste it into Penny, and customize the underlined/bracketed parts to fit your specific situation. |  |  | 
# | Concept Area | Prompt Text | What Good Looks Like (Coach Reference)
1.0 | Objects & Data Model | I'm just learning Salesforce. Can you explain the difference between an Account and a Contact like you're talking to someone who has never seen a CRM before? Then give me one example from a nonprofit context. | Penny explains Account as the organization/household and Contact as the individual person, with an example like 'Martinez Household' containing Carlos, Maria, and Sofia.
2.0 | Objects & Data Model | What is the difference between a lookup relationship and a master-detail relationship in Salesforce? Which one should I use if I want to see a Contact's Cases but also want the ability to delete the Contact without losing the Cases? | Penny explains that master-detail deletes child records when parent is deleted (lookup does not), and recommends lookup for Cases-to-Contact.
3.0 | Objects & Data Model | I keep hearing the term 'NPSP Household Account Model.' In plain English, how does NPSP change the standard relationship between a Contact and an Account in Salesforce? | Penny explains that standard Salesforce links Contacts to business Accounts, while NPSP auto-creates a 'Household Account' (e.g., 'Martinez Household') that groups family members together.
4.0 | Field Types | I'm building a custom field to track whether a client is a veteran. My options are a Text field, a Picklist, or a Checkbox. Walk me through the pros and cons of each, and then tell me which one the Salesforce Admin exam would consider best practice and why. | Penny explains Picklist enforces controlled values (Yes/No/Unknown), Checkbox only allows Yes/No (no Unknown state), and Text is too freeform. Best practice = Picklist because it enables clean filtering and reporting.
5.0 | Field Types | Why does it matter what field type I choose when I'm just storing data? Can't I put almost anything in a Text field? | Penny explains that field type affects how data can be filtered, reported on, imported, and validated — and that using Text instead of Picklist breaks reports, import matching, and future automation.
6.0 | Security | I keep hearing the words OWD, Role Hierarchy, Profiles, and Permission Sets in Salesforce training. Can you give me a one-sentence plain-English definition of each one, and then explain how they work together in one short paragraph? | Good answer: OWD = default sharing baseline; Role Hierarchy = grants upward visibility; Profiles = what you can do; Permission Sets = additions on top of profiles. Together they form layered access control.
7.0 | Security | If I set Case OWD to Private, what exactly does that mean for who can see a case record? And how does the Role Hierarchy change that? | Penny explains: OWD Private means only the record owner can see it. Role Hierarchy means people above the owner in the hierarchy also gain visibility — so a Program Director can see a Caseworker's cases.
8.0 | Exam Prep | I'm studying for the Salesforce Platform Foundations certification. I just finished reading about the standard Salesforce data model. Can you give me three practice questions the way they'd appear on the actual exam, and then explain the correct answer and why the other options are wrong? | Penny provides scenario-based questions with 4 answer options each, explains the correct choice, and identifies why the distractors are plausible but wrong.
9.0 | Exam Prep | I scored 65% on my Platform Foundations practice test. I got most of the data model questions right but struggled with the security questions. Can you give me a focused review of the 3–4 most important security concepts I need to know for this exam? | Penny focuses on OWD, Role Hierarchy, Profiles, FLS, and Sharing Rules — the core security stack that appears on the exam.
10.0 | Documentation | I found a Salesforce Help article but I can't tell if it applies to my situation. The article title is: [paste title here]. What 3 questions should I ask myself to figure out if this article is relevant to what I'm trying to do? | Good Penny response prompts: (1) Does it mention the same feature/object I'm working with? (2) Does it match my edition/org type? (3) Does the scenario described match my use case?
11.0 | Documentation | I'm stuck on a Salesforce configuration problem and I've read the Help article twice and I still don't understand it. Before I ask my coach, what steps should I take to try to solve it myself? | Good answer: (1) Restate the problem in one sentence; (2) Search Trailhead for a related module; (3) Ask Penny to explain the concept in a different way; (4) Try it in the org and observe what happens; (5) Then escalate.
12.0 | Decision Log | I just made a configuration decision in my Salesforce org that I need to document. The decision was: [describe what you decided]. Can you help me write a strong Admin Decision Log entry that explains what I chose, why I chose it, and what the alternative was? | A strong Penny-assisted entry includes: what was decided, why that option (business reason + technical reason), what the alternative was and why it was rejected, and any downstream implications.
13.0 | Portfolio | I need to write a professional headline for my Salesforce portfolio site. I'm a career-changer with a background in [previous field]. I just earned my Platform Foundations certification and I'm working toward my Admin certification. Can you suggest 3 headline options that would appeal to a hiring manager? | Good headlines position the learner's prior background as an asset, emphasize real org work, and avoid generic phrases like 'passionate about Salesforce.'
